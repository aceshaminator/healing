# 🌿 Healing by VisionAce

> **Healing** is the flagship health and accessibility platform developed under **VisionAce**. Designed specifically for low-resource and offline environments, Healing delivers accessible health tracking, device-sensor diagnostics, localized emergency support, and multi-language accessibility straight to users without requiring active internet connectivity.

## ✨ Key Features

* 📡 **Offline-First Architecture:** Built to operate seamlessly without an active internet connection, storing data safely on the local device.
* 🫀 **Sensor-Based Health Checks:** Uses hardware camera and motion sensors for fingertip pulse detection and hand steadiness evaluation.
* 🌍 **Multilingual & Accessible UI:** Full language toggling (English, French, Hausa, Igbo, Yoruba) paired with built-in screen reader assistance and text-resizing tools.
* 📋 **Doctor Summary Generator:** Aggregates readings, symptoms, and medical notes into printable PDF or text summaries for clinical visits.
* 🚨 **Emergency Protocol:** Localized emergency contact numbers and actionable crisis management tools.
